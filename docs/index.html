How to read this page

  • Market prices are not predictions made by this project. They are prices set by traders on Polymarket and Kalshi, shown as they are.
  • Nomination odds are the average of the Polymarket and Kalshi prices for “wins the party’s 2028 nomination”, using the midpoint between the best buy and sell offers. The Democratic and Republican contests are separate markets, so each party’s odds sum to about 100% on their own.
  • The 30-day lines each have their own vertical scale, so compare their shapes and not their heights. The 7-day change column gives the size of the move.
  • Announces by end of 2027 is the Polymarket price that the person officially announces a 2028 presidential run by December 31, 2027. Not every candidate has this contract.
  • Polls counts national primary polls from the last 30 days, taken from Wikipedia’s poll lists, that name the candidate, with the candidate’s average share in those polls.
  • Wikipedia views is the average number of daily views of the candidate’s English Wikipedia article over the last seven days.
  • Who is listed: anyone priced at 1% or more for a nomination on either market, or named in three or more primary polls in the past year, as of November 12, 2026.
  • Office is the office each person holds, or last held, once the officials elected in November 2026 are seated in January 2027.
  • Events are entered by hand from news coverage, each with a source link.

Data from Polymarket, Kalshi, the Wikimedia pageviews API, the Federal Election Commission, and Wikipedia (text available under CC BY-SA 4.0). The page is rebuilt once a day.
